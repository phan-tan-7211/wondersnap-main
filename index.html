    WonderSnap

    Glowing 3D wonders, the human body (brain, beating heart, breathing lungs, eye, ear, skeleton…), DNA, a cell, engines, vehicles and machines made of up to a quarter-million light particles — controlled by your hand in front of the webcam.

    1. Snap your fingers to summon the particles.
    2. Make a fist to form a wonder, engine or car.
    3. Open your hand: wonders morph to the next one — organs, engines and the car explode to show every part inside, named with what it does. Close your hand to put it back together.
    4. Twist your hand to turn the model, raise or lower it to tilt; two hands zoom.
    5. Point ☝ at a part to learn what it does, pinch 🤏 to pull it out. Try the 🎓 quiz, 🎤 voice, ✂ cut-away and ⏺ recording.
    6. Show ✌ to jump to the next model.
